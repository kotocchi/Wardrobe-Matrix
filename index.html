Our Styling Philosophy

Good styling isn't random—it's driven by visual physics. We use these 7 core principles to optimize optical length ratios (avoiding 1:1 body cuts), create fabric tension (pairing rigid jackets over soft bases), and frame bold accent colors with dark outerwear so every combination looks intentional and well-proportioned.

Evaluation Concepts 1 / 7
1. Rule of Thirds

Balances 1:2 volume ratios (e.g., fitted or cropped tops paired with wide-leg bottoms) to optically elongate your leg length.

2. Tone-on-Tone

Harmonizes pieces from the same color family using contrasting shades and saturations (e.g., sky blue top + raw navy denim).

3. Pan (Structural Shift)

Pairs sharp, structured jackets over soft, relaxed base layers to prevent all-soft outfits from looking like loungewear.

4. WI / Wit (High-Low)

Blends formal, tailored garments (like dress trousers) with casual streetwear basics (like graphic tees) to remove stuffiness.

5. Pointing Down

Grounds bright, bold accent colors underneath dark outerwear so the focal point stays centered and balanced.

6. Damin Look

Evaluates minimalist, oversized neutral-tone silhouettes (cream, charcoal, earth tones) to keep loose fits clean and elevated.

7. Go Baseline

Ensures effortless, proportion-driven balance for simple single-layer fits when you don't need outer layers.
